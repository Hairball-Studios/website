Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Hairball Studios is an indie game studio in Kentucky, USA. This page explains what personal information this website collects and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you email us asking to join the launch-news list.
  • Anything you send us, if you email one of our contact addresses.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this site.

How we use it

We use your email address only to send you launch news from Hairball Studios. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address on the launch-news list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Where it is handled

Hairball Studios is based in the United States, and the services listed below may store and process information in the United States and other countries.

Who handles it for us

  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub privacy statement) serves this site and, like any web host, may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Email: messages sent to our contact addresses, including launch-news signups, are handled by Google Workspace, which is also where we keep the list.

Your choices

Every launch-news email we send tells you how to unsubscribe. You can also ask us at any time to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@hairballstudios.gg.

Children

The signup is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will remove the address.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
